# M2 and M3 v2 closure

Date: 2026-07-10  
Status: Historical record

The background inspector now extends beyond processes into units, cgroups, sockets, timers, mounts, namespaces, bounded event recording, exact inventory export, and five TUI views. Real Arch/Hyprland inventory, performance, recording, interaction, and terminal-signal tests were completed before closure.

The KISS constraint survived: one binary, no daemon, no telemetry, no setuid, no automatic sudo, no database, and no third-party Rust crates.
