## Status vocabulary

- **Proposed:** open to discussion.
- **Accepted:** current baseline; change by a new superseding record.
- **Superseded:** retained historical record, replaced by a named later record.
- **Rejected:** considered and retained with rationale.
- **Verified:** supported by a repeatable experiment or cited authoritative source.

Unless marked otherwise, the initial documents are **Proposed**. The M0/M1 closure records freeze what was actually delivered; future corrections follow the append-only policy.
